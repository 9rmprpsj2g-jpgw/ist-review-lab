# v1 convergence audit — nonzero finding; stop gate

Refitted all 5,880 SVM fits across the archived 105 runs (75 SVM runs; 30 non-SVM runs). Eight fits emitted ConvergenceWarning, all on C15, across six runs: fixed_20 four, uncertainty three, auto_tar one. Every flagged fit reported n_iter_=10,000. No warnings occurred on C12, C16, C18 or GCRIM, or in frozen_svm/explore_10. Non-SVM policies perform no fits.

The checker imports the byte-identical learner from v1-frozen. It uses the original single RNG stream, original separate seed-selection RNG, original training order and original hard-coded model parameters. Runtime versions and source-data digest match v1. Labels are supplied from archived prefixes; each newly selected batch must match the archived batch exactly before continuing. All 105 trajectories and fit counts matched. Temporary-negative IDs were never archived in v1; they are reconstructed from the frozen code and deterministic RNG consumption, not recovered from a historical negative-ID log. The per-fit ledgers store their reconstructed identities, actual parameters, n_iter_, warnings and the labeled prefix/archive digest. Absence of a warning is the recorded solver convergence status, not an independent mathematical optimality certificate.

The 90% overshoot comparison includes a trajectory with an upstream non-converged fit: C15 / seed 11 / fixed_20, fit 209 at 4,161 reviewed documents; the stored 90% crossing is 4,283 and committed effort is 4,301. The other flagged auto_tar/fixed_20 fits occur after their corresponding 90% measurement. No flagged fit in those arms precedes the stored 75% or 80% targets. This identifies which measurements used affected trajectories; it does not determine whether completing optimization would change their values. No counterfactual rerun or Phase 4 analysis was performed.

Convergence issues therefore existed at the original 5,000-document budget. The proposed claim that they appeared only at census depth is not supported. The full-grid rerun, empirical max_iter search, permanent instrumentation changes and paper-comparison amendment are deferred at the user's nonzero-v1 gate. No max_iter, C, loss, dual, tol or historical result was changed. The failed census generation remains FAILED; no replacement generation exists yet, so it has not been labeled superseded by one.

Files: check.py is the diagnostic driver; source/learner_v1.py is frozen source; report.json contains per-run identity, convergence counts, exact-match status and ledger digests; findings.json and nonconverged_fits.csv identify failures and relation to persisted targets. JSONL ledgers live outside git under audit_store/v1_convergence_check and are included in the downloadable evidence ZIP.

Contemporaneous corroboration: original results/run.log contains eight ConvergenceWarning messages (line references and log digest in findings.json). These were missed in earlier review; convergence was not entirely silent. The raw parallel-run log did not record fit identities or n_iter_. Attribution in this report comes from the exact retrospective reconstruction, not inferred ordering of parallel log messages.
